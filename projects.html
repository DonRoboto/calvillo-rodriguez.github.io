Projects & Research

My ongoing research focuses on scalable meta-optimization frameworks and their application to complex, dynamic environments.

Closed-Loop Online Diagnostic-Aware AutoRL

Design and implementation of an automated reinforcement learning framework. Benchmarked across various MuJoCo locomotion environments to evaluate performance and adaptability in continuous control tasks.

Bayesian Swarm-Based Training

Developed a population-based meta-optimization algorithm that leverages swarm intelligence and Bayesian optimization to enhance hyperparameter tuning efficiency.

Cost-Aware Graph Reinforcement Learning for Epidemic Containment

Applied advanced graph neural networks and RL algorithms to model and optimize dynamic network containment strategies under budget constraints.
