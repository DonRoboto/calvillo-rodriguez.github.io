About me

I am an AI Researcher and Computational Economist specializing in Applied AI and the analysis of Complex Systems. By combining advanced machine learning frameworks with economic principles, I develop scalable, automated algorithms designed to optimize decision-making and continuous control in highly dynamic environments.

I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from the Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México (UNAM) and serve as a Lecturer at Tecnológico de Monterrey. My current focus is bridging the gap between theoretical computational economics and practical, real-world artificial intelligence applications.

Core Expertise

My technical work operates at the intersection of these key domains:

  • Automated Reinforcement Learning (AutoRL): Designing closed-loop, self-tuning algorithms for continuous control tasks.
  • Meta-Optimization: Leveraging Population-Based Training, Swarm Intelligence, and Bayesian Optimization for hyperparameter scheduling.
  • Complex Networks: Applying Graph Neural Networks to dynamic environments, such as cost-aware topological immunization.
