About me

I am a Lecturer at Tecnológico de Monterrey and a recent Ph.D. graduate in Computer Science and Engineering from the Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México (UNAM). My work covers the design, analysis, and application of scalable meta-optimization frameworks.

My research bridges computational economics, complex networks, and artificial intelligence, specializing in algorithms for dynamic environments and continuous control tasks.
