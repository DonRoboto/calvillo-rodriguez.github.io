About Me

I am a Lecturer at Tecnológico de Monterrey and a recent Ph.D. graduate in Computer Science and Engineering from the Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México (UNAM).

My research bridges computational economics, complex networks, and artificial intelligence. I specialize in designing scalable meta-optimization frameworks—such as Bayesian Swarm-Based Training and Closed-Loop Online Diagnostic-Aware AutoRL—for dynamic environments and continuous control tasks.
